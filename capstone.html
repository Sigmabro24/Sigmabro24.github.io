Capstone Idea

My capstone is a platformer game that is 2d made in gamemaker studio 2 and would only allow you to go up as there is some mysterious darkness following you. It will have a health system and a boss maybe a dialogue system too, it will have an eerie style and the higher you go the more clear the area gets.

So for the plan I would watch youtube videos for the functions like gravity and health system I would also need to do the black smoke thing that follows you as well also gamemaker documentation: https://manual.gamemaker.io/ and also map hazards like spikes and stuff which would require to understand collisions, also tilesets so maybe a video of that Education plan.

Capstone plan in order in terms of importance

  1. Gamemaker tutorial
  2. Collision tutorial
  3. Gravity tutorial
  4. Healthbar tutorial
  5. Follow player tutorial
  6. Enemy basics
  7. Room transition
  8. Tileset
  9. Powerups
