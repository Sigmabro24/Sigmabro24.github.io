Semantic tags are tags that describe what they do to the coder and browser making them incredibly useful.

Tags Definition
Header Used to introduce content in a html page
Main Usually goes in the middle and is meant for the primary stuff on a page.
Footer Meant for legal things on a html page and is meant for the bottom.
Section A group of things that share a similar idea.
Article A semantic tag for content that can be by itself (individual content).
Aside Related to article but contains content that isn't a part of the main idea.
Picture Semantic tag meant for images and allows for multiple images and various sizes mostly depending on device size.
Nav Meant to assist with navigation and usually is on the top or on the side of a page.
Figure Meant for self-contained content, usually with a caption.
